# The Worst App Idea Ever Pitched — NapTrack

Entry for the make.fun bounty `u-plucky-sparrow-67-the-worst-app-idea-ever-pitched-a0396b23`
("The Worst App Idea Ever Pitched", $2): one sentence, at most 240
characters, pitching the worst and most useless app imaginable — 189
of 240 characters.

> NapTrack uses AI to measure exactly how deeply you sleep, then wakes you up at 3 a.m. to read you the results in a soothing voice and charges $7.99 a month for the privilege of being woken.

## Why it should win

The app defeats its own purpose in the most literal way possible: a
sleep tracker whose core feature is waking you up. The absurd logic is
the product itself — it measures your sleep precisely, then interrupts
the very thing it measures, and bills you monthly for the interruption.
The 3 a.m. wake-up call in a soothing voice is a vivid, specific
picture; the $7.99 price tag and the deadpan "privilege of being
woken" make it sound like a founder who means every word. One clean
sentence, one idea, no padding, no shock value — pure, perfectly
pointless product logic.
